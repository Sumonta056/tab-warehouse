Privacy policy

Tab Warehouse has no server, no account, no analytics and no tracking. The developer never receives your data.

Last updated: 23 October 2026

What the extension uses

  • Bookmarks. The extension reads and changes your Chrome bookmarks only to show and organise them. They stay in Chrome.
  • Preferences. Sidebar order, categories, environment rules, the Home look, custom icons and the Home background image are saved in chrome.storage.local on your computer.
  • Current tab. When you open the toolbar popup, the extension reads the title and URL of the current tab so that you can save it as a bookmark.

What leaves your computer

The extension sends no bookmarks, titles, full URLs or settings to any server. It loads only these images from the network:

  • Official Google app icons (Gmail, Drive and others) from ssl.gstatic.com.
  • An icon URL that you add yourself to a Home link.
  • The icon of a Home link that Chrome has no icon for. For this, the extension asks Google's favicon service (www.google.com/s2/favicons) and sends it only the host name of that link, for example github.com. Local addresses (localhost, IP addresses, names without a dot) are never sent.

These requests follow Google's privacy policy.

What the developer does not do

  • Collect, store or sell personal data.
  • Share data with third parties, or use it for advertising or credit decisions.
  • Use data for any purpose other than the bookmark features of the extension.

Permissions

bookmarks Read and edit your bookmarks, the core of the extension.
storage Save your preferences on your computer.
favicon Show site icons from Chrome's own icon cache.
activeTab Read the current tab's title and URL, only when you open the popup.
tabs See which saved links are open in a tab, to mark them "Open" in the list. The addresses stay in memory and are never saved or sent.

Remove your data

Remove the extension at chrome://extensions. Chrome then deletes its preferences. Your bookmarks stay in Chrome.
